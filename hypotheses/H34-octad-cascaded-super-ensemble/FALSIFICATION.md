1. **Utility Regression:** Octad-Pareto Utility falls below 0.4350 (failing to exceed standalone HTC-Net's 0.4405).
2. **Macro AP Regression:** Macro AP drops below 0.5750.
3. **Precision Collapse:** Macro Precision drops below 0.6000.
