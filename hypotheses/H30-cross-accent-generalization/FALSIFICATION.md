# Falsification Criteria: Hypothesis H30

## 1. Primary Falsification Gates
Hypothesis H30 will be considered **FALSIFIED** if:
1. **Severe Accent Variance:** Cross-accent variation $\sigma_{\text{L1}}(\text{Macro F1}) > 0.0800$, indicating acute accent overfitting.
2. **Subgroup Collapse:** Any single L1 native language cohort exhibits Macro F1 $< 0.4500$ or Macro Precision $< 0.5000$.
3. **Severe False Warning Asymmetry:** False Warning Rate exceeds $3.00$ false alarms/utterance on any individual L1 cohort.

## 2. Inconclusive Conditions
1. Accent variance is low ($\sigma < 0.05$) only because performance is uniformly degraded across all groups.
