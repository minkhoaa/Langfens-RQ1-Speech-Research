## Abstract
This paper describes a method for automatically assessing the pronunciation of a second-language learner at the phone level. The core algorithm calculates a Goodness of Pronunciation (GOP) score for each individual phone by taking the logarithm of the posterior probability that the learner produced the target phone given the acoustic segment. The acoustic observations are modeled using standard Hidden Markov Models (HMMs). Normalization is achieved through an unconstrained phone loop recognition. GOP scores provide objective, phoneme-level diagnostic feedback for mispronunciation detection without requiring human rater scoring.
