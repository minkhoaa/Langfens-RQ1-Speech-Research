### Tested non-CTC family

A small supervised speaker-adversarial gradient-reversal model was trained using frozen audio features, intended-phone conditioning and grouped TRAIN folds. It is an exploratory analogue, not a reproduction of Dao et al. or Spin. Exact-gap event-OOF AP changed .162125→.165887 (+.003762 concatenated events), while fold-mean changed .163123→.162838 (−.000284) and 3/4 folds declined. No reliable generalization gain is supported.

### Source and evidence separation

Claims about published mechanisms and their reported test corpora are from the cited original sources; they are not validated LANGFENS performance. Local experimental facts and limits are detailed in `reports/METHOD_BENCH_03_C_GENERALIZATION_AND_RESEARCH.md`, `reports/METHOD_BENCH_03_A_PHONEME_CTC.md`, and `reports/METHOD_BENCH_03_B_PROBABILISTIC_EDIT.md`.
