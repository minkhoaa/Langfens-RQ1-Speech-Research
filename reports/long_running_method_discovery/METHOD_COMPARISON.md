## Cross-cutting protocol

Use raw ranking metrics and calibrated policy metrics separately. Report AP S/O/I, exact N+1 gap coverage, precision/recall/F1, FWR, low-FP partial PR/top-K, per-speaker results with uncertainty, and short-phone false positives. No pooled speaker-count pseudo-replication. DEV_SEARCH remains exploratory; reserve independent speakers/data for final claims.
