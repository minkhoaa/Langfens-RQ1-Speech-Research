## 3. Synthesis rules and residual uncertainty

- Speech-native precedents justify research eligibility, not measured Langfens superiority. No common deletion AP, FPR, feedback or runtime comparison exists across these papers.
- Three strongest actionable evidence seams are (i) explicit acoustic sequence alternatives/scoring, (ii) existing realized-phone recognition + reference comparison as a control, and (iii) compact speech-native error/realization supervision. Tagging is one proposed parameterization, not a privileged proven architecture.
- Full graph normalization/terminal/repeated-label correctness, scale/occupancy handling, licensing, calibrated acoustic grounding, checkpoint changes, small-speaker generalization and feedback quality remain unverified.
- Do not turn paper/table availability into code clearance, hypotheses into facts, compatibility into strict resume or rubric rank into empirical performance. All future experiments require separate authorization.
