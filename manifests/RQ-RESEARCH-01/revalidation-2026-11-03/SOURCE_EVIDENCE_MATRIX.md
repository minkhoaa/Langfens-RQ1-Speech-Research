## Coordinator corrections to historical/raw lane assertions

- Preserve raw independent reports, but do not inherit their arithmetic. Ranking in the integrated report is centrally recomputed with one rubric.
- A's provenance addendum is authoritative over any suggestion of untrained E1. Its best-AP .3156 versus fixed-endpoint AP comparison is not a fair like-for-like drift test; fixed training history endpoint .309018 versus benchmark .309293 is the closer comparison and still not reconciled here.
- A's old citation to an uninspected 2007 GOP paper/DOI is excluded; original thesis names Witt & Young (2000), DOI `10.1016/S0167-6393(99)00044-8`. Its primary full text was not retrieved here. Use inspected GOP definitions, not the questionable 2007 attribution.
- D conflates 2017 monotonic attention with 2018 MoChA. Neither establishes absence confidence or a tested Langfens edit lattice.
- A raw likelihood ratio is **not universally biased toward shorter strings** under CTC. Candidate-set/path multiplicity and duration/activation effects must be specified and calibrated. S1 supplies a concrete normalized graph formulation; arbitrary length division is not automatically a valid posterior.
- A learned skip mass can be near zero after actual training; this does not prove direct skip supervision works. Calling an extra auxiliary loss “two-head compatible” concerns module count only, not compliance with the unchanged two-loss contract.
- Retrieval's 104.08% PER is verified from the paper but does not justify rejecting all retrieval as deletion-blind. Its lack of direct omission results justifies low confidence, not an impossibility proof.
- Pretrained recognizers/AED/RNN-T are not inherently thesis violations. Rebuilding general ASR or foundation training is out of scope; costs and narrow task fit must be assessed specifically.
