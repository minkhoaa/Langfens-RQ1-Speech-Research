## Directly inspected primary speech evidence

1. **Fu et al. (2021), A Full Text-Dependent End to End Mispronunciation Detection and Diagnosis with Easy Data Augmentation Techniques**, [arXiv:2104.08428v1](https://arxiv.org/abs/2104.08428). Full primary PDF inspected. Audio queries attend to canonical-phone representations, followed by CTC realized-phone recognition; acoustic residual limits excessive text influence (§2). This is the opposite attention direction to Langfens's canonical-query MDD head. Text augmentation includes substitutions and blank-related insert/delete modifications, not natural waveform deletion synthesis. Table 3 reports aggregate F1 49.29→56.08 but recall 74.78→56.12 and PER 27.75→15.58 for the cited best system. Better recognition/aggregate F1 therefore does not establish better omission sensitivity. Table 4 contains 918 gold deletions: correct deletion diagnoses 499/918 (54.36%) for baseline versus 482/918 (52.50%) for the reference-aware model; diagnosis errors decline, but correct diagnoses do not improve. Different speaker split plus TIMIT training prevents transplanting numbers to Langfens.
   - **Implementation inspected:** [cageyoko/CTC-Attention-Mispronunciation](https://github.com/cageyoko/CTC-Attention-Mispronunciation), README, tree and `egs/attention_aug/models/model_ctc.py`. CNN/RNN, text embedding/BiLSTM, acoustic-query dot-product attention and phone output are present. No repository license metadata was returned; code availability is not permission to copy or evidence of reproduction. No author weights/runnable environment verified.
2. **Zhang, Wang & Yang (2022), End-to-End Mispronunciation Detection with Simulated Error Distance**, [primary paper](https://www.isca-archive.org/interspeech_2022/zhang22p_interspeech.pdf), §§2–3. Direct target-conditioned binary error prediction plus an auxiliary realized-phone recognition loss is actual speech evidence for joint learning. SSL-unit replacement and a distance target are not a supervised natural-deletion classifier. MFA is used to aggregate augmentation distances into phone targets; XLS-R, clustering and external native/unlabeled data increase reproduction and split-isolation costs. The paper's 8:1:1 annotated-data split is not Langfens's frozen fourfold contract. [Author demo](https://zju-zhan-zhang.github.io/e2e-sed/) is referenced by the paper; executable implementation/license/checkpoint not verified here. No omission AP demonstrated.

## Competing methods and actual local changes

| Candidate | Substitution/deletion and supervision | Change seams; costs; feedback |
|---|---|---|
| Audio-query reference-aware CTC | Recognizes realized phones with prompt support; omissions inferred through sequence edits. Realized sequence already available; no gold time needed at runtime. | Different attention direction/fusion in `model.py`; retain phone CTC. Extra prompt pathway modest relative to encoder but latency/training unmeasured. Risks copying canonical text and suppressing errors; confidence needs alignment ambiguity. |
| Compact canonical-token operation classifier | Predict KEEP/SUB/OMIT from acoustic and reference evidence; optional replacement identity only for valid substitutions. Parsed labels provide operations, but collator currently discards deletion identity. | `textgrid_parser.py`, `dataset.py`, `collate.py`, `model.py`, losses and evaluator integration would change under a separate proposal. Low extra head FLOPs; same encoder; unknown total T4 cost. Clear feedback semantics, but no proven gain over binary BCE or acoustic shortcut resistance. |
| Joint recognition + error/existence objective | Keep CTC realized-phone branch, train token error/existence evidence jointly; distinguish absence from wrong realization. | Existing frozen encoder/CTC plus token branch is an efficient reuse point. Auxiliary losses change the objective even if the number of heads stays two; authorization required. Can overfit position/phone priors; omission target alone does not ground attention. |
| Explicit edit transducer/lattice | Reference-advancing epsilon = omission; competing emission = substitution; extra emission = insertion. Requires normalized paths and correct operation direction. | More DP/transition and calibration code than compact tagging. A standard RNN-T blank alone does not encode deletion of a reference token. Full recognizer replacement deferred for cost/validation burden, not blanket scope rejection. |

`model.py:434–449` freezes the diagnosis head under the current two-head contract; a 39-phone replacement classifier is not currently trained diagnosis. `dataset.py:97–153` excludes deletion from realized CTC and masks deletion diagnosis. `alignment.py:73–205` is gold-canonical/G2P reconciliation, not an existing acoustic edit transducer. Reusing that routine without auditing direction/tie-breaking would be unsafe.

Canonical→realized OMIT corresponds to restoring/INSERTing the expected phone in pedagogical correction. Never invert this convention or call a G2P mismatch a learner omission. An absent phone has no realized identity; confidence/timestamps must support uncertainty rather than fabricate a segment.
