# Lane B — Acoustic realization and phoneme recognition (independent audit)

**Disposition: GO for research/pilot consideration only; no execution authorization.** Of the compared families, existing frozen-backbone CTC realization analysis is the most compatible first diagnostic, not an established omission detector. CTC’s blank, frame-phone posteriors, and greedy phone sequence are acoustic signals but not direct labels for “the expected phone is absent.” A reference-conditioned classifier is needed for target-specific error decisions; a free-phone recognizer avoids text priors but incurs alignment/operation ambiguity. The negative E1 benchmark argues against repeating skip-token attention as-is. No model, weights, audio, labels, or sealed TEST partition were accessed; no code/tests/training were run.

## 4. Operation direction, identifiability, and uncertainty

Define canonical/reference sequence `r` from prompt G2P or trusted reference variants and realized acoustic phone sequence `h` from acoustic evidence. **Substitution** means one expected `r_i` has a corresponding realized phone `h_j != r_i`; **deletion** means `r_i` has no corresponding realized phone; **insertion** means a realized phone has no expected reference counterpart. Never reverse the alignment operation by naming “gold phone absent from G2P” a learner deletion: G2P/reference mismatch is not a speech error. In `alignment.py:73–91, 146–205`, the code aligns gold/reference against G2P and uses specific `deletion` semantics for a gold phone unaligned to G2P; that offline lexical reconciliation must not be confused with acoustic deletion. Word-pronunciation variants (reduced forms, stress, dialect) make one canonical sequence insufficient without variant alternatives.

CTC optimizes a sum over latent paths that insert blank and repeat labels then collapse; blank means “no phone emitted at this frame in this path,” not “canonical phone was omitted in the utterance.” Framewise argmax, blank fraction, and weak phone posterior all vary with neighboring phones, speaking rate, noise, coarticulation, and alignment. At 50 Hz, a 20 ms frame is similar to many short-phone intervals; boundary and interval annotation errors can dominate. The local DIAG-02B matched result is a small-cluster exploratory association, not causality. Free recognizer output can be wrong even when target production is acceptable; reference-conditioned scoring can improve relevance while creating expectation/LM error-erasure. Report operation-level precision/recall/AP and calibration/coverage, not only PER/WER or threshold F1. Thresholds must be selected on validation only and separately from reported test values; never use gold TextGrid windows at inference.

## 6. Risks, complementarity, confidence and gaps

**Complement:** a CTC realization score can be one independent acoustic feature for a reference-conditioned verifier; a phonological attribute head may explain substitution articulations, but cannot alone identify absence. Product’s current word-ASR path is neither candidate evaluator nor suitable gold source. Avoid blending ASR and acoustic score into one number without calibration/ablation.
