## Accounting semantics

- AP is non-interpolated grouped-tie AP, calculated only on finite answered members of each explicitly named score/population. Binary and legacy omission/substitution use `s_error`; dedicated omission/substitution use their own `s_omit`/`s_sub` for `operation_ce` and `class_focal`; both objectives require these three-way channels. They are unavailable only for explicitly `binary_collapsed` records. Score families are never interchanged.
- CAL thresholds use CAL correct-only scores, inclusive `score >= threshold`, with tied negatives not split. The .05/.10/.15 ceilings are computed independently per score. CAL is not VAL tuning. Threshold results include achieved CAL FPR and full-census confusion.
- Operational omission confusion uses the entire canonical gold census. It reports TP, `FP_correct`, `FP_sub`, `FP_omission`, `FP_other_subtype`, correct FN/TN and other-subtype TN, aligned recall, all-gold recall (abstained true errors remain missed), answerable/all-gold correct-token FPR bounds, and false alarms per 1,000 of all gold positions. AP still excludes substitutions for legacy omission comparability. Extra prompt alarms are a separate ledger and never silently included in gold metrics.
- Coverage, actual operation-label counts, speaker and canonical-phone strata are calculated from the saved rows. VAL speaker IDs must be exactly NJS/RRBI; CAL exactly NCC/THV; sealed TEST speakers fail validation. Gold keys and prompt keys are unique and runtime/offline joins are exact. Saved greedy-CTC edit/reference counts produce PER and edit subtypes, expressly distinct from human omission/substitution counts.
- Genuine Z record validation requires 9,956 actual gold rows with label counts 8,799/1,012/145, 9,919 mapped rows with labels 8,774/1,008/137, and separately counted 37 unmapped gold rows and 37 extras. For each available omission operating point `FN=145-TP` and `TP<=137` are enforced. These are validation checks, not inserted count corrections.
- Primary omission precision/recall/FA scalars use dedicated `s_omit` at the registered CAL 15% threshold, not legacy `s_error` or the 10% point. Contrasts include binary AP, dedicated omission AP, omission precision/recall/FA, legacy and dedicated substitution AP, PER, and matched-minus-mismatched binary AP acoustic gap. Matched factorial contrasts are T1-C0, T2-C0, T3-C0 and interaction T3-T2-T1+C0.
- The frozen Protocol Z gates are computed separately per cell on fixed-budget only: Level A precision>=.25, all-gold recall>=.30, FA/1k<=30, binary AP>=.313619, legacy substitution AP>=.303974, PER<=.246980; Level B precision>=.30, recall>=.40, FA/1k<=15, the same noninferiority thresholds, and matched-minus-mismatched binary AP>=.010. Missing endpoints produce unavailable, never pass. For `best`, absolute gates are not applicable. These external-reference gates remain distinct from internal C0 contrasts.
