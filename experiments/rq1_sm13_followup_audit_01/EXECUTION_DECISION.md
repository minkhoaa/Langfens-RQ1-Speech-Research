# SM13 vowel/stop follow-up: decision after source and model checks

**Date:** 2026-11-13  
**Decision:** Keep the frozen SM13 policy and checkpoints unchanged. The two training-only changes, the explicit seven-window temporal feature pilot, and the 51D specialist nested-transfer check all fail the grouped operating gate. The next work should establish the intended feedback target through blind listening and test genuinely new acoustic/context evidence under a preregistered low-warning gate.  
**Confidence:** High for the empirical frozen-score limit and raw-label semantics on TRAIN; moderate that simple loss/specialist changes are insufficient; low for any claim about which internal representation layer causes the failure.

## Was most earlier work caused by missing analysis at the start?

No such broad conclusion is supported. The prior [SM13 reproduction and method decision](../../reports/RQ1_METHOD_SELECT_02R/FINAL_METHOD_DECISION.md) found a real **9.2–10.6% relative Macro-3 F1 gain at matched realized FWR** over the Phase-01 comparator. Thus prior rounds did establish aggregate improvement. The gap was that this aggregate gate did not require a minimum precision and recall **for vowel and stop substitutions separately**. A model can improve Macro-3 while yielding zero operating recall in these groups. The subsequent diagnostic also contained factual errors: wrong confusion counts, a mistaken inference that equal normalized phones meant annotation anomalies, and a wrong SM13 head size. Those errors matter for choosing the next experiment; they do not invalidate every earlier benchmark.

## Next executable gate

1. Two independent listeners fill [the blind 136-clip review sheet](AUDIO_REVIEW_BLIND.csv) using [the protocol](AUDIO_REVIEW_PROTOCOL.md). This determines whether the desired feedback target agrees with corpus `*` labels, and identifies any systematic disagreement before relabeling or further training. No AI-generated or automatic judgment should be entered as a human vote.
2. Preregister one **new evidence** hypothesis around phone-specific vowel contrasts and stop closure/release context, using the [missed-phone concentration](ERROR_REGION_REPORT.md). The tested seven-window mel recipe has already failed its low-warning gate. Before running, fix the candidate, folds, and gate: ≥15% recall and ≥50% precision **for each** vowel and stop substitution group at realized joint FWR ≤0.20, with unchanged O/I semantics. Report confidence by speaker and the full TP/FP counts. The historical [EXP-68 temporal-pyramid review](../EXP-68-MULTI-RESOLUTION-TEMPORAL-PYRAMID-NET/REVIEW.md) is prior work to inspect before reimplementing waveform features; its reported metrics are not a matched SM13 comparison.
3. If a candidate passes grouped TRAIN development without selecting thresholds on held speakers, confirm once on a genuinely new speaker cohort. `TEST_SEALED` and `DEV_REPLICATION` have already been exposed in historical work and cannot serve as fresh final holdouts.

The current work has completed the source reconciliation, corrected the diagnostic report, prepared a blind listening packet, executed two isolated SM13 model pilots, a controlled temporal feature pilot, and a nested specialist transfer check. It has **not** delivered a validated performance fix or independent human listening judgments.
