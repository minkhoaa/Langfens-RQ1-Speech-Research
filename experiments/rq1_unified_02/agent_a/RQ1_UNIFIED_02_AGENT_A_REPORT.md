## Empirical observations (Q1–Q3)

1. **Frozen branch substitution isolates the insertion branch as a major limitation.** On the identical 19,755 target / 20,354 exact-gap DEV_SEARCH population, U2 target + U2 gap yields AP-S/O/I = .37136/.18532/.11799, Macro-3 error AP .22489. Keeping those exact U2 target scores fixed and substituting the matched Gold-Only gap scores preserves AP-S/O exactly and raises AP-I to .18672 and Macro-3 AP to .24780 (+.02291). Substituting the EXP43 gap score (diagnostic composition; not a unified checkpoint) gives AP-I .28506 and Macro-3 AP .28058. This is direct evidence that U2 target scores are not responsible for U2's weak insertion AP in these fixed-score comparisons; it does not establish a deployable architecture or causal training explanation.
2. **The gap head has a severe in-sample/generalization gap.** Re-scoring cached U2 seed-101 features on CPU gives train exact-gap AP .97698 (560/61,362 positive), versus DEV_CAL AP .13811 (102/10,343) and DEV_SEARCH AP .11799 (125/20,354). In-sample train ROC-AUC is .99986; calibration and search ROC-AUC are .81530 and .78343. The same trained checkpoint and frozen features are used throughout. This is strong evidence of overfit / poor transfer for this head, but does not identify whether data scarcity, label noise, or a representation mismatch is causal.
3. **Insertions are overwhelmingly internal, while boundary slots are too sparse to estimate.** On DEV_SEARCH, 124/125 insertion positives are internal (internal AP .12788); leading gaps have 0 positives in 599 slots; trailing gaps have 1 positive in 599 slots (AP .3333 from one positive is not informative). Thus an aggregate exact-gap AP is dominated by internal slots, and separate estimates for utterance boundaries are inconclusive.

## Feature construction and geometry

`train_twohead.py::prepare_gap_context` constructs a 1,614D vector per exact N+1 gap: 768D mean of offsets 11:16 from the previous intended-phone crop, 768D mean of offsets 1:6 from the next phone crop, and one 39D intended-phone encoding for each adjacent reference phone. Missing side is represented by an all-zero acoustic and phone vector. No realized-phone labels, gold boundaries, or gap labels enter the features. This represents the edges of neighboring H-002 phone crops, not a directly sampled shared boundary window.

On a deterministic 10,000-row TRAIN sample, left and right acoustic blocks each have an estimated covariance effective rank around 236/768, top eigenvalue about 3.9% of variance and top 10 about 25.6%. The 39D intended-phone blocks have effective rank around 29.4/39. These block-level diagnostics show nontrivial but redundant feature variation; they do not prove the embedding lacks insertion information.

## Hypotheses and limits

- **Supported:** U2 exact-gap scores are much weaker out of sample than in sample; replacing only the gap scores raises macro error AP substantially; the SEARCH insertion positives are concentrated in internal slots.
- **Refuted for the matched seed-101 evidence:** the claim that merely replacing U2 target scores fixes insertion ranking (target-only replacement leaves AP-I unchanged); the claim that the 1,614D feature has trivial variance (estimated effective ranks are materially above zero).
- **Inconclusive:** why the gap head overfits, whether a boundary-window representation improves held-out ranking, leading/trailing boundary performance, and G0–G9 comparisons. DEV_SEARCH is exploratory and one seed is not evidence of population-general benefit.

No gold timing or realized-phone data was used as a feature. No held-out performance claim is made.
